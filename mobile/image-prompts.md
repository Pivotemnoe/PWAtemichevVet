Four images were generated with the built-in Image Gen tool on 2026-10-13. These are the production briefs; exact original files and optimized exports are recorded in `asset-provenance.json`. The original TemichevVet logo and existing photograph of the doctor were reused without generative changes.

1. **Companions.** Premium editorial wide photograph for a mobile veterinary pet-care app. A friendly golden retriever and silver tabby cat together in an airy home, ivory and pale blue palette, soft daylight, realistic fur. Animals toward the right, negative space on the left. No people, text, logos, medical instruments, or invented clinic setting. Display as a small card with rounded corners.
2. **Health.** High-quality small 3D clay illustration of a friendly cat and dog beside a blue-and-white health clipboard, subtle coral heart. Simple readable silhouette for a mobile action tile. Transparent background, no text or logo; match the blue, ivory and coral palette.
3. **Food.** Matching high-quality 3D clay illustration of a blue pet bowl with kibble and friendly cat/dog companions. Compact readable silhouette, transparent background, no text or logo.
4. **Dates.** Matching 3D clay calendar with blank cells, blue notification bell and a small coral paw detail. Clear silhouette suitable for a mobile date/reminder card. Transparent background, no text or logo.

Optimized WebP exports: one 1200×634 photograph, three 420×420 transparent illustrations. Original generated PNGs are preserved outside the repository; exports are copied into `web/assets/mobile/`.
